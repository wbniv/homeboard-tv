HOMEBOARD TV · ANDROID TV / GOOGLE TV

Quick start & user guide

Your rain, dates, air quality and favorite apps, together on one dashboard.

1 · Install Homeboard

  1. Finish the TV’s normal setup and connect it to your home network.
  2. Open biohack.net/homeboard on your phone or computer → Download APK. Requires Android TV / Google TV, Android 8.0+.
  3. Copy it to the TV using a USB drive (if supported) or a TV file-transfer app. Open the APK in a TV file manager.
  4. If prompted, allow Install unknown apps for that file manager, then choose Install. Menu names vary by TV. Turn that permission off afterward.
  5. Open Homeboard TV from Apps. You can use it without changing your default launcher.

Optional: Home settings → Default Home app → choose Homeboard in Android’s prompt. Some TVs’ Home buttons still open their original launcher.

Updates: install a newer public APK from the same page over the public app. If rejected, keep the installed app to preserve its dashboard and settings; check that the download is a newer public release. Earlier debug builds are separate apps; their settings do not transfer.

Connect for optional cleaning

  1. Open Homeboard’s setup companion on your computer. Keep the computer and TV on the same trusted network.
  2. Follow its connection instructions. If asked, enable the TV’s developer options and wireless debugging. Approve the computer on the TV; enter a pairing code only in the companion’s pairing prompt.
  3. Check the displayed TV name, model and identity before continuing. Repeat the connection process for each TV.

The setup companion is separate from the phone dashboard editor. You can use the dashboard without cleaning anything.

What cleaning changes

Cleaning disables only the apps you select for your TV user. It keeps their data and records their original states for restoration.

Homeboard, launchers, casting, streaming, remote controls, networking and essential system services are kept. Uncertain items stay unchanged.

2 · Review and clean your TV

  1. In the companion, choose your connected TV and Inspect this TV. Inspection changes nothing.
  2. Review the suggested apps and their purposes. Nothing starts selected. Select only items you want disabled; leave anything you use unchecked.
  3. Review the final list and target TV. Save the restore point and keep its recovery copy on your computer. Apply remains unavailable if the TV is unsupported or recovery is not ready.
  4. Choose Apply selected changes and confirm the listed items. Keep the connection open while each result is verified. Use Stop to stop after the current command.
  5. Check the results, then try your remote, favorite streaming apps, casting, sound and inputs. Restore a changed item if something you use stops working.

Cleaning never means uninstalling every suggested app, clearing data or resetting the TV. A TV update can re-enable apps; inspect again before making further changes.

Restore changes

  1. Open the companion’s saved cleanup session for the same TV.
  2. Review its changed apps. Restore one item or the whole session.
  3. Wait for verification. Check any unresolved items before considering recovery complete. Apps disabled before cleaning keep their original state.

Lost connection? Reconnect to the same TV and choose Check result before retrying. A missing reply does not mean a change succeeded or failed.

Homeboard won’t open? Use the computer companion and saved recovery copy; restoration does not depend on the TV dashboard being open.

Keep recovery files until you are satisfied with the TV. Finish the companion connection when done; keep recovery access available until any unresolved result is settled.

3 · Learn five controls

ArrowsMove focus. Right past Home opens All Apps; Back there returns Home.
Press OKExpand a widget or launch an app. On the clock, open the meeting planner.
Hold OKConfigure, move or resize a widget; open an app’s actions.
Press BackClose details or cancel a preview. From a full-screen widget, restore the dashboard.
Hold Back
1 second		On Home or All Apps, undo one saved change. Release before undoing another.

Top-right sliders = Homeboard settings; gear = Android settings. Full-screen widgets also have a visible Restore dashboard button.

4 · Arrange your dashboard

World clock · Settings buttons Rain · next 24 hours CalendarAir quality Favorites · YouTube · one scrolling row

Hold OK on a widget → Move or Resize. Arrows adjust the preview; OK saves, Back cancels.

Home settings → Widgets / Restore adds widgets or restores the Default dashboard. An empty Home still allows Right to All Apps.

To add a favorite: All Apps → hold OK on an app → Favorite. Wedges show more apps inside Favorites; OK launches the focused app.

5 · Configure from your phone

  1. Use the same trusted home network. On the TV: Home settings → More → Configure from phone.
  2. Scan the QR code, match the connection code and choose Allow on the TV. Keep Homeboard open.
  3. Tap a widget to edit it. Use Arrange dashboard to move/resize; drag Favorites rows by their grips to reorder.
  4. Review the draft → Update [TV name]. Finish with Done editing in the TV-name menu.

Phone icon per TV: TV-name menu → Add TV to home screen. A fresh TV session and approval are still required.

6 · Make each widget useful

Rain: next 24 hours by hour, then six days. Allow TV location, or hold OK → Location for a city, address or coordinates. Ranges and units are configurable.

Calendar: OK opens four weeks and day details. Sources & offline data shows coverage, last checks and refresh. Published holidays are cached; Wan Phra/flower dates calculate offline, validated for 2024–2027. Missing coverage is shown explicitly.

Air quality: hold OK to choose location/source. Check the timestamp and label: hourly model estimates differ from station 24-hour averages. OK opens the map.

Timers & alarms: add a widget for named cooking timers or one-off date/time alarms. OK opens Pause, +1 minute and Stop; paired phone controls work too. Grant alarm access on the TV. Sound over other apps requires a powered, audible TV.

World clock: hold OK to edit cities; its position beside Settings is fixed.

Change your mind or get unstuck

Undo: hold Back on Home, or open Home settings → Undo history. Removing a favorite/widget does not uninstall its app.

Stale data? Check timestamps and source status; new rain/air data needs internet. Saved data remains during failures. If location fails, choose a fixed place.

Settings: choose language, colors and Widget background opacity. Thai dates use Buddhist Era years.
