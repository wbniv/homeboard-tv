HOMEBOARD TV · ANDROID TV / GOOGLE TV

Quick start & user guide

Your rain, dates, air quality and favorite apps, together on one dashboard.

1 · Start on a new TV

  1. Complete the TV’s normal setup and connect it to your home network.
  2. Enroll through the device coordinator’s Add a device flow; have Homeboard installed through that service. No Play Store release yet.
  3. Open Homeboard TV from the TV’s app list. You can try it without changing the default launcher.

Optional: Home settings → Default Home app. Some TVs’ Home buttons still open their original launcher.

2 · Learn five controls

ArrowsMove focus. Right past Home opens All Apps; Back there returns Home.
Press OKExpand a widget or launch an app. On the clock, open the meeting planner.
Hold OKConfigure, move or resize a widget; open an app’s actions.
Press BackClose details or cancel a preview. From a full-screen widget, restore the dashboard.
Hold Back
1 second		On Home or All Apps, undo one saved change. Release before undoing another.

Top-right sliders = Homeboard settings; gear = Android settings. Full-screen widgets also have a visible Restore dashboard button.

3 · Arrange your dashboard

World clock · Settings buttons Rain · next 24 hours CalendarAir quality Favorites · one scrolling row

Hold OK on a widget → Move or Resize. Arrows adjust the preview; OK saves, Back cancels.

Home settings → Widgets / Restore adds widgets or restores the Default dashboard. An empty Home still allows Right to All Apps.

To add a favorite: All Apps → hold OK on an app → Favorite. Wedges show more apps inside Favorites; OK launches the focused app.

4 · Configure from your phone

  1. Use the same trusted home network. On the TV: Home settings → More → Configure from phone.
  2. Scan the QR code, match the connection code and choose Allow on the TV. Keep Homeboard open.
  3. Tap a widget to edit it. Use Arrange dashboard to move/resize; drag Favorites rows by their grips to reorder.
  4. Review the draft → Update [TV name]. Finish with Done editing in the TV-name menu.

Phone icon per TV: TV-name menu → Add TV to home screen. A fresh TV session and approval are still required.

5 · Make each widget useful

Rain: next 24 hours by hour, then six days. Allow TV location, or hold OK → Location for a city, address or coordinates. Ranges and units are configurable.

Calendar: OK opens four weeks and day details. Sources & offline data shows coverage, last checks and refresh. Published holidays are cached; Wan Phra/flower dates calculate offline, validated for 2024–2027. Missing coverage is shown explicitly.

Air quality: hold OK to choose location/source. Check the timestamp and label: hourly model estimates differ from station 24-hour averages. OK opens the map.

World clock: hold OK to edit cities; its position beside Settings is fixed.

New-TV cleanup: inspection only

No real app cleaning yet. TV: Home settings → More → TV cleanup · Inspect only.

On the setup computer: task cleanup:serve → choose the enrolled TV → Inspect this TV. Reports list hardware, firmware and package count. Apply stays unavailable pending device-specific cleanup review and restore proof.

Change your mind or get unstuck

Undo: hold Back on Home, or open Home settings → Undo history. Removing a favorite/widget does not uninstall its app.

Stale data? Check timestamps and source status; new rain/air data needs internet. Saved data remains during failures. If location fails, choose a fixed place.

Settings: choose language, colors and Widget background opacity. Thai dates use Buddhist Era years.
