# Get wallpaper providers

On the TV, open **Settings → Appearance → Background → Get providers**.
Three cards fit each page. Use Previous/Next wedges and Back; no scrollbar is
needed. Each card separates installation status from the specific test evidence.

Overflight, Aerial Views and Movie Art link to their verified Google Play listings.
For an app not installed on this TV, open its card and select **Open Google Play on
this TV**, or scan the QR code with your phone. In Google Play on the phone, choose
the eligible TV yourself. Pairing with Homeboard does not select that TV in Play.
If the TV store cannot open, the QR code and full address remain available.
Availability depends on your account, region and the publisher.

Return to Homeboard to refresh the selected card, or use **Check installed apps**.
Installed cards offer **Configure provider** when accessible and **Preview installed
provider** when their v1 service is discoverable. Preview does not save anything;
choose **Use background** to save, or Back to cancel. Hold Back to Undo a saved
change. Installing never activates a provider or changes your default launcher or
system screensaver. Existing provider settings and app data are preserved.

The phone controller has **Get wallpaper providers** on its dashboard and TV menu.
It names the connected TV and shows the same directory. Links open another browser
tab; directory browsing does not change the dashboard draft. Configure/Preview
remain on the TV. No third-party APK is bundled, downloaded or installed by Homeboard.

Community links lead to publisher instructions/releases on the phone, under
Advanced. They are not Google Play listings. Backend-dependent and source-only
projects remain in the [testing plan](../plans/2026-11-18-provider-testing-spike-and-installation.md)
until a usable setup path has been established.

Weather Wallpaper v2.11 has an exclusion explanation and no install action.
Keep Play Protect enabled; see the [security review](../testing/2026-11-18-weather-wallpaper-security-review.md).
Store presence does not establish safety or compatibility. Baseline tests are
partial compatibility, and a GitHub edition result does not verify the Play edition.

See the [screenshot verification record](../testing/2026-11-18-provider-directory.md)
for actual captures and remaining store-round-trip limits.
