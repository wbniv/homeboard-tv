# Cooking timers and event alarms

Add **Timers & alarms** from Home settings → Widgets → Add widget, or from the
paired phone controller. Start with **2×1**; resize it to show more dishes.
Press OK to open all timers; Back or Restore dashboard returns to the same place.

For cooking, choose a quick minute preset or enter a dish name, minutes and seconds.
Each preset starts immediately. You can run several timers, Pause/Resume each one,
add one minute, or Stop it. Restart uses its original duration. Stop then Remove
clears an old entry. You can keep up to 24 entries.

For an appointment or household reminder, switch to **Event alarm** and enter its
name, date and time. The screen shows the **TV timezone**; the paired phone uses
that timezone too. For example, set separate alarms for Shower at 12:00, Call Grab
at 12:15 and Leave for school at 12:30. Past times are rejected; nothing silently
moves to tomorrow. Event alarms are once only. Date entry uses CE/ISO; Thai date
summaries use the Buddhist Era.

On Android 12 or later, **Allow alarms on this TV** opens Alarms & reminders access
when needed. After granting it, return to Homeboard and start the timer. Allow
notifications as well to get the Stop alert sound notification action. Homeboard
uses a bundled original alarm tone even over another app; it never opens itself automatically,
changes your volume, or unmutes the TV. A sound lasts at most one minute; Done
remains visible until you Stop or Restart. Multiple finished timers retain their names.

The TV must be powered and its alarm audio audible. Check a short test timer on
your TV before relying on it. Manufacturer restrictions, force-stop, denied
permissions, audio routing and a muted/off TV can prevent audible delivery.

Running cooking timers survive app recreation on the same boot. After a TV reboot,
they say Interrupted and require explicit restart. Future dated alarms are restored;
an alarm missed while powered off says Missed, without a surprise late sound.
Changing the clock or timezone does not change cooking durations. Dated alarms
retain their chosen instant and display the TV's current local time.

Phone timer actions happen immediately, separately from layout Apply/Undo. A lost
response offers **Retry timer action** using the same command identity, so it cannot
add time twice or create two timers. Existing timers keep running if your phone
disconnects or you remove the widget. Reopen Timers to manage them.
