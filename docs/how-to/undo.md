# Undo on the TV

On **Home or All Apps**, hold **Back for one second**. The banner shows the change
and hold progress, then confirms **Undid [change]**. Release the button; it undoes
once per hold. Hold again with a fresh press to undo the preceding change.

A short Back press keeps normal navigation. Inside a menu, editor or temporary
preview, Back keeps its cancel/close behavior; return to Home or All Apps before
holding Back to undo a saved change.

The visible **Undo** button remains available: press Down to reach it, then OK.
It has a focus highlight and stays visible while selected. Up or a short Back
returns focus to content. The ordinary banner lasts 20 seconds.

For earlier changes, open **Homeboard Settings → Undo history**. The list is
newest first. Selecting an older entry also reverses the newer entries above it.

**Appearance → Widget background opacity** changes card fills. It leaves
wallpaper/video brightness and independent wallpaper dimming unchanged. Done
saves one undoable change; Back cancels its unsaved preview.
