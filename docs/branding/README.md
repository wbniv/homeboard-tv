# Homeboard TV artwork

The launcher icon and TV banner use a white television mark containing turquoise,
light-blue and amber widget tiles on midnight navy. These are Homeboard assets,
not CleanYourTV branding. Both masters were generated using the built-in image
generation tool; ImageMagick resized the selected images for Android packaging.

- Icon master: `homeboard-icon-master.png`
- Banner master: `homeboard-banner-master.png`
- Packaged icon: `../../launcher/app/src/main/res/mipmap-nodpi/ic_homeboard.png` (192 × 192)
- Packaged TV banner: `../../launcher/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/homeboard_banner.png` (320 × 180)

## Icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Android launcher icon for Homeboard TV, an independent customizable TV widget dashboard. Create one polished square app icon, no text. A bold minimal television outline containing three softly rounded dashboard tiles: a tall turquoise tile on the left, two smaller pale blue and warm amber tiles stacked on the right. Deep midnight navy full-bleed square background. Flat crisp graphic with very subtle depth, no photo, no mockup, no extra objects, no letters or watermark. Center the entire TV mark in the middle 60 percent of the canvas so circular Android masks cannot clip it; generous even margins. Highly recognizable at 48 pixels. Output square.

## Banner prompt

Create an Android TV launcher banner for Homeboard TV, wide landscape EXACT 16:9 composition. Use the previously generated Homeboard television-with-three-widget-tiles icon as the brand reference, preserving its white TV shape, turquoise, light blue and amber tiles. Place a smaller version of that mark at the left, then clear large white sans-serif text on the right in two lines reading exactly 'Homeboard' and 'TV'. Deep midnight navy background throughout, flat graphic, generous margins, no other words, no mockup, no watermark. This is the actual launcher banner asset, crisp and readable when displayed at 320 by 180 pixels.
