What happens if you turn this on

  • When someone buys from a Homeboard shopping button, some shops pay Homeboard a commission. Your household gets half, paid to PromptPay.
  • Leaves the house: a random reward code inside shopping links you tap, and your PromptPay number, stored by Homeboard's payout service in Thailand.
  • Never leaves: your lists, what you watch, which apps you open, or who is home.
  • No ads. Turning this on shows no adverts. Sponsored offers are a separate choice and are not available.
  • Off unless you turn it on. Stopping takes one press, and you are paid everything owed.
