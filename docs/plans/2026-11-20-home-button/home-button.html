Homeboard · proposed design · Home settings → Home button on Google TV, three states

Home settings · Home button

Off (the default)
Home button opens HomeboardOnOff

A short press of Home opens Google TV Home. Turn this on to open Homeboard instead. Google TV Home stays installed, and holding Home still opens its quick settings and home panel.

Google TV Home keeps the Home button even when another Home app is chosen, so Homeboard uses its helper instead.

The helper is an Android accessibility service. It sees remote button presses and which app is in front so it can catch Home. It reads nothing on screen and sends nothing anywhere.
To give Home back at any time: turn this off, or hold Home and turn off “Home button” in Android's accessibility settings. Uninstalling Homeboard also gives Home back. Google TV Home itself is never disabled or changed.

On and working
Home button opens HomeboardOnOffWorking

A short press of Home opens Homeboard from any app. If the home screen of Google TV Home appears, Homeboard replaces it. Hold Home for its quick settings and home panel.

Working means Android has the helper running now, not only that it was asked for.

The helper is an Android accessibility service. It sees remote button presses and which app is in front so it can catch Home. It reads nothing on screen and sends nothing anywhere.
To give Home back at any time: turn this off, or hold Home and turn off “Home button” in Android's accessibility settings. Uninstalling Homeboard also gives Home back. Google TV Home itself is never disabled or changed.

On, but the helper is not running
Home button opens HomeboardOnOffNot running

The setting is on, but Android is not running Homeboard's Home button helper, so Home opens Google TV Home. Android stops it when Homeboard is force-stopped, and sometimes after an update.

Android lists the helper as on but has not started it. Start it again, or turn it off and on in Android's accessibility settings.
