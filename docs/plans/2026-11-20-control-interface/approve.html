Allow “Home Assistant” to control this TV?

From192.168.1.20 on your home networkCode482 913 · matches this TV

It will be able to

  • show a Home page, a full-screen widget or the screensaver
  • start, pause and stop timers and alarms
  • choose the screensaver and when it starts
  • read timers, PM2.5 and what Homeboard is showing
It will not be able to change your dashboard, open other apps, or add other devices. You can remove it at any time in Control from other devices.
After Allow: back in the list, “Home Assistant · Connected” is focused, with the Undo banner “Added Home Assistant · hold Back to undo”.
After Deny, Back, or 2 minutes: nothing is added; the add window stays open with a new code so the right device can try again.
Shown only while the add window is open in front of you. Leaving Homeboard closes the window and refuses a waiting device.
