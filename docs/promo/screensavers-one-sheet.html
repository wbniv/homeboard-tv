HOMEBOARD TV ANDROID TV & GOOGLE TV · IN DEVELOPMENT

Make idle time your own.

Choose a quiet clock, your Home background or an installed Android screensaver. Preview and configure your choice from the sofa.

Two built-in choices

Clock shows the time and date over a glow, your Home background or black. Home background fills the screen, with an optional clock. Dim the backdrop to suit your room.

Try it, then make it yours

Open Home settings → Screensaver. Preview a built-in choice, press OK to use it or Back to cancel. Saved changes support Undo.

Choose when it starts

Let Android decide, or choose 1, 2, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes on Home. Any remote key returns to Home; a ringing timer or alarm opens Timers & alarms.

Use an installed app

Android screensaver apps appear in the list. Open their settings when available; select Use everywhere to let Android run one across apps.

Use everywhere: A one-time developer permission is required. Homeboard asks before taking control, keeps the original Android screensaver and offers Restore. Android controls the idle timeout.

Current status: Built-in screensavers and Home idle behavior have Cast1 evidence. The full permission-granted Use everywhere workflow still needs device acceptance. Wallpaper providers as screensavers, a screensaver directory and phone controls are planned.
