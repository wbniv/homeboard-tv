The original GeoJSON's polygon rings were retained, with longitude/latitude
rounded to three decimal places and properties removed. The resulting
world-clock-land.json is drawn with an equirectangular projection.

Natural Earth states that its raster and vector map data are public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Made with Natural Earth. No network map service is used by this widget.
